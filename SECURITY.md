# Security Policy

This KYN-000C public-beta source is not deployed and must not contain real
participant data or production secrets. Do not open a public issue containing a vulnerability that could
expose people, credentials, evidence, graph relationships, signing keys, or ballot
linkage.

The proposed public-beta private reporting address is `kc@uspartyparty.com`, with a
three-business-day acknowledgement target and seven-business-day initial-status
target. The exact candidate process is published at `/kyn/security` in the Party
Party portal. It becomes operative only with explicit KC release acceptance; until
then, no real-data deployment is authorized.
