# ADR 0002: Anonymous Participant, Accountable Authority

- Status: accepted architecture direction

Ordinary participants use participant-controlled keys and pairwise identifiers with
no mandatory direct PII. People who voluntarily accept verifier, adjudicator,
custodian, or operator authority accept stronger proofing and public accountability.
Authority is claim-specific, capped, expiring, audited, challengeable, and never a
universal social score.
