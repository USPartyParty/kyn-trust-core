# ADR 0004: Entity, Project, And Operator Map

- Status: accepted architecture direction; supersedes ADR 0001
- Accepted by: KC Streich
- Accepted on: 2026-07-30

Five Letter Agency operates KYN as a component of The Party Party public project.
The Party Party is an FLA project/product name, not a separate legal entity,
organization, contracting party, or source of campaign authority.

The Korey Streich Campaign Committee is KYN's separate campaign relying party. AI
for Wisconsin is the Committee's public-facing campaign program name, not another
legal entity.

The FLA-operated KYN trust domain and Campaign Committee relying-party domain retain
separate keys, stores, access, authority, records, notices, and financial treatment.
That technical separation does not misrepresent public names or project contexts as
legal organizations. Common leadership and collaboration create no implicit data
access, authority, payment, intellectual-property transfer, or credential sharing.
Real-data or paid operation requires the applicable written agreements, notices, and
reviews.
