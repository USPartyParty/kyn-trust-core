# KYN Gate B Operator And Relying-Party Agreement

- Version: `1.0.0`
- State: accepted for the bounded KC-first Gate B package
- Accepted: 2026-08-23
- Independent legal opinion: no

## Parties and public names

Five Letter Agency is the KYN operator. It operates KYN within The Party Party public
project. The Party Party is a project/product name, not a separate legal entity.

The Korey Streich Campaign Committee is the separate relying party. It uses AI for
Wisconsin as its public-facing program name. AI for Wisconsin is not a separate legal
entity.

KC Streich's common leadership of the operator and relying party is disclosed. It
does not combine their authority, funds, credentials, data, or records.

## Gate B purpose

The operator may activate exactly one KC-controlled, expiring,
`bootstrap_vouched` KYN subject under release `1.0.0`. The relying party may later
request only an exact, claim-specific, minimum-disclosure presentation through the
separately reviewed Campaign Core boundary.

Gate B does not open ordinary enrollment, admit sensitive evidence, verify Wisconsin
residency or elector status, create a campaign account, authorize a poll or ballot,
make a campaign decision, or create a candidate obligation.

## Data and custody

KYN retains participant keys, private attestation relationships, authority grants,
recovery state, challenges, and protocol audit records inside the FLA-operated KYN
boundary. The Campaign Committee must not receive raw KYN evidence, the private
relationship graph, a reusable KYN subject identifier, or KYN operator credentials.

A relying-party presentation is pairwise, purpose-bound, versioned, expiring, and
limited to the exact assurance basis required by the accepted action. A future ballot
service receives a ballot-scoped authorization or nullifier rather than KYN identity.

KYN uses a separate encrypted backup repository and credentials. It must not enter
the Campaign Committee backup repository. KC/FLA is the initial recovery custodian;
recovery material must not live only on the Optiplex.

## Security and privacy

Release `1.0.0` keeps sensitive-evidence collection disabled. The one-subject
provisional storage exception permits the disclosed unencrypted state volume only
after a separate encrypted backup and isolated restore pass. A backup failure,
restore failure, unexpected exposure, separation failure, or unauthorized subject
stops the beta.

Participant export, correction/invalidation, consent withdrawal, deletion,
challenge, appeal, revocation, and recovery remain governed by their exact signed
commands and public notices. Logs and public evidence remain content-free or
redacted as required.

## Authority and financial boundary

Operating KYN creates no Campaign Committee authority for FLA and no FLA authority
for the Campaign Committee. This agreement creates no payment, contribution,
reimbursement, purchase, property transfer, fundraising channel, or related-party
transaction. Any later financial or service arrangement requires its own exact
classification, approval, valuation, record, and reporting.

The KYN operator cannot cast a participant vote, exercise campaign authority, or
turn a credential into statutory proof. The relying party cannot inspect KYN's
private graph or direct an attestation merely because it relies on a presentation.

## Duration and change

This agreement controls only Gate B release `1.0.0` and one KC subject. Material
changes require a new version and applicable operator, campaign, privacy, security,
financial, or legal review. Ordinary enrollment and Gate C are separate decisions.
